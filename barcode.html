Generate a Product Barcode

This is a Code128 barcode (not a plain UPC/EAN) — it can hold a full link, not just digits. Scanning it with any barcode/QR reader app opens the product page directly, same idea as the QR version.

555 is the Parle-G entry already in products.json.

Why not a real EAN-13 like DMart uses: a real retail barcode only stores a 12–13 digit number — no room for a URL. DMart's scanner reads that number and looks up price/name in their own internal database, which outside apps can't access. This Code128 barcode is a fair stand-in for a classroom demo because it's still a genuine, scannable barcode format — just one built to carry a link instead of a plain ID.
